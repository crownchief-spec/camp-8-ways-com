Karaoke quiet-hours reminder

Karaoke may be used until 10:00 PM. When the other tent has guests, the quiet period begins at 10:00 PM and continues until check-out at 12:00 noon the next day, so karaoke is not available during that period. Full-site private bookings do not have a time limit, but guests should still keep the volume considerate.
