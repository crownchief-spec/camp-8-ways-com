Equipment Layout

Four Grill Burners + One Side Burner

  • Lower cast-iron grate: the main cooking surface for meat, vegetables, and other food.
  • Upper warming rack: keeps cooked food warm. Do not mix raw and cooked food.
  • Right-side burner: place a stable pot in the center for soup, hot pot, or sauce.
  • Lid and thermometer: close the lid for preheating and more even cooking.

Quick Lighting

The Gas Knobs and Ignition Button Work Separately

  1. Open the lid first: never light the grill with the lid closed.
  2. Check that all knobs are OFF: if you smell gas, do not press the ignition button. Shut off the gas supply and contact the host.
  3. Turn on the selected burner: push in its large knob and turn it to the lighting/high position.
  4. Press the black IGNITION button: release it after the burner lights.
  5. Light additional burners one at a time: each large knob controls its own burner.
Remember: the large knobs control gas and heat. The center black button creates the ignition spark.

Preheat First, Then Choose Direct or Indirect Heat

1. Preheat with the lid closed for 10–15 minutes

After the flame is stable, close the lid and preheat for about 10–15 minutes. This heats the cast-iron grate evenly and helps reduce sticking. Adjust the heat before adding food.

2. Direct heat for thinner, quick-cooking food

Place food over a lit burner. This works well for sliced meat, burgers, vegetables, and other food that cooks quickly. If grease causes a flare-up, lower the heat or move the food to an unlit area.

3. Indirect heat for thick or slow-cooking food

Light the burners beside the food, leave the burner directly below it off, and close the lid. The circulating heat cooks thick cuts and delicate food more evenly.

4. Right-side burner for soup or hot pot

The far-right knob controls the side burner. Center the pot securely, keep handles away from walkways, and never leave it unattended.

Shutdown and Safety

  1. Turn all five large knobs to OFF.
  2. Shut off the gas supply and confirm that every flame is out.
  3. Do not touch or pour water onto the hot grate, lid, or side burner.
  4. Let the grill cool completely before cleaning. Keep children away and never leave a lit grill unattended.
If ignition fails: turn the knob back to OFF, keep the lid open, and wait at least five minutes before trying again. Stop and contact the host if you smell gas or the burner still does not light.
