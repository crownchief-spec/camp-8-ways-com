American-Style LPG BBQ Grill Guide

Each guest area has one KS14074 American-style BBQ grill supplied by an LPG cylinder. Use the main cooking area for grilling and the right-side burner for a stable pot.

Before Lighting

Open the Lid, Turn Knobs Off, Check for Gas

  • Use the grill outdoors in a ventilated area and keep combustible items at least 1 metre away.
  • Open the lid and make sure every burner knob is OFF.
  • If you smell gas, do not ignite the grill or operate electrical switches. Close the cylinder valve and contact the host.
  • The host connects the regulator, hose, and cylinder. Guests should not disconnect or modify them.

Quick Lighting

Follow These Five Steps

  1. Keep the lid open and confirm that all burner knobs are OFF.
  2. Open the LPG cylinder valve.
  3. Push in one main-burner knob and turn it anticlockwise to MAX.
  4. Press and hold the black IGNITION button for up to about five seconds. Confirm that the burner has lit.
  5. Light any additional burner one at a time. Keep the lid open and preheat on high for about 3–5 minutes, then reduce the heat for cooking.
If ignition fails: turn every knob to OFF, close the cylinder valve, keep the lid open, and wait at least five minutes. Stop and contact the host if it still will not light or you smell gas.

While Cooking and Using the Side Burner

  • Side burner: open its lid first, then push in its knob, turn to MAX, and press IGNITION. Centre the pot securely and keep handles out of walkways.
  • Stay nearby: never leave a lit grill unattended. Keep children and pets at a safe distance.
  • Prevent grease fires: trim excess fat and avoid repeatedly brushing on oil. A solid griddle must not cover more than half the cooking area.
  • Avoid burns: the lid, grate, handle area, and side burner become very hot. Use long tools and heat-resistant gloves.

Shutdown and Emergencies

  1. Close the LPG cylinder valve first and wait for the remaining gas in the hose to burn off.
  2. After every flame is out, turn all burner knobs to OFF.
  3. Let the grill cool completely before cleaning the grease tray and grate. Never pour water on a hot grill.
Never use water on a grease fire: move everyone away. If safe, close the cylinder valve, keep the grill lid open, and use a dry-powder fire extinguisher. Evacuate and call 119 if the fire is not immediately controlled.
