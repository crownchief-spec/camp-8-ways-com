Automatic Mahjong Table Guide

Press the two red buttons together to raise or lower the center tile tray and start automatic shuffling. The East, South, West, and North seat buttons activate the electronic dice.

Quick Steps

Use Both Red Buttons Together

  1. Press both red buttons together: the round center tile tray rises.
  2. Push the tiles into the opening: load all tiles and keep fingers clear of the moving tray.
  3. Press both red buttons together again: the tray lowers, automatic shuffling starts, and the prepared tiles rise onto the table.

Please note: Keep hands away from the opening while the tray moves, and do not place drinks or other objects in the shuffling area.

How Do the Electronic Dice Work?

The center panel has controls for the East, South, West, and North seats. Press a dice button at any seat to roll and display the points.

Remember two things: press both red buttons together for the center tray and automatic shuffle; use the four seat buttons for the electronic dice.
