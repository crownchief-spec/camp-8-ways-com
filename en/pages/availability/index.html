How to read the calendar

For one night, check the check-in date only. Example: check in Jul 17, check out Jul 18 → look at Jul 17.

For two nights, check each overnight date from check-in. Example: check in Jul 17, check out Jul 19 → look at Jul 17 and Jul 18.
