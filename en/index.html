Two Private Tent Areas, Two Different Styles

Joyforest currently has two main private dome tent areas.

Balloon Tent is more suitable for families, friends, outdoor meals, BBQ-style gatherings, birthday celebrations, and guests who want a larger outdoor social space.

Cloud Tent is more suitable for couples, small families, quiet stays, indoor movie time, forest-view bathtub moments, and guests who prefer a slower vacation feeling.

Balloon Tent

Private Lawn, Outdoor Kitchen, and Forest Gathering Atmosphere

The Balloon Tent is designed for guests who want a fuller outdoor gathering experience. It is especially suitable for families, friends, outdoor dining, birthday celebrations, BBQ-style meals, and guests who want to spend more time on the private lawn.

This area includes a dome tent, private lawn, outdoor kitchen, outdoor dining area, and a warm night atmosphere with string lights.

Outdoor Kitchen

A small outdoor kitchen area for preparing meals, hot pot, BBQ-style food, breakfast, drinks, and snacks.

Dome Tent Stay

A comfortable dome tent with bed, air-conditioning / heating, indoor resting space, and basic facilities.

Outdoor Dining Atmosphere

The Balloon Tent area is especially suitable for outdoor meals, family dinners, friend gatherings, and celebration-style stays.

Night String Lights

At night, the lawn and outdoor area become warmer and more atmospheric with string lights.

General Notes

Shared reminders for all guests. This does not replace the Balloon Tent or Cloud Tent check-in guides above.

Meals

Meals and ingredients are not included. Please buy food, drinks, breakfast, and snacks before arrival.

Nearby Shopping

There are supermarkets, convenience stores, and local restaurants around the Yangmei area. We recommend buying supplies before check-in.

Quiet Hours

Please lower your voice and music volume at night. Joyforest is a quiet private stay, not a loud party campsite.

No Driving on Grass

Please do not drive any car onto the lawn.

No Smoking Indoors

Smoking is not allowed inside the tent.

Fire Safety

Please be careful when using BBQ, fire, gas, or cooking equipment. Never leave fire unattended.

Children

Children are welcome, but parents must watch them carefully around slopes, stairs, fire, kitchen equipment, and outdoor areas.

Self Check-in

Joyforest is a self check-in stay. There is no 24-hour front desk. Please read the instructions before arrival.
