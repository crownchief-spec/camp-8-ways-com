    Joyforest is a private forest glamping stay and outdoor event venue in Yangmei, Taoyuan, Taiwan, near Taipei. Guests can choose Balloon Tent or Cloud Tent for a private dome tent accommodation experience with lawns, outdoor kitchens, BBQ, night lights, projector, bathtub, and self check-in. Joyforest is also suitable for birthdays, baby milestone parties, proposals, pet gatherings, family photography, graduation photos, pet photography, BBQ gatherings, karaoke, outdoor movies, and private outdoor events in northern Taiwan.
